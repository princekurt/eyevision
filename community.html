Community Care

Practical, age‑appropriate eye care tips and resources for kids, adults, and seniors — helping everyone see clearly and stay healthy.

Kids

Look out for squinting, headaches, sitting too close to screens, or difficulty reading at school.

Tip: Encourage outdoor time and follow the 20‑20‑20 rule for screens.

Early checks help a lot

Adults

Digital eye strain is common. Blurry vision, dry eyes, and fatigue can improve with updated lenses.

Tip: Optimize screen brightness, reduce glare, and consider blue‑light filtering if appropriate.

Comfort for work and life

Seniors

Regular checkups help monitor age‑related changes. Don’t ignore sudden shifts in vision.

Tip: Keep prescriptions updated and ensure good lighting for reading and stairs.
